Biography

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. Dacheng Tao, since July 2024. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in July 2023, supervised by Associate Professor Xian-Ling Mao, and my Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering from the same university in June 2018.

My research interests include hashing for image and cross-modal retrieval, vision-language pre-training, and efficient generative models.
