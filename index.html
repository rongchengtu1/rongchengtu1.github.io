Biography

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. Dacheng Tao, since July 2024. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in July 2023, supervised by Prof. Xian-Ling Mao, and my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Technology from the same university in June 2018.

My research focuses on efficient information retrieval and inference acceleration for video generation models and large language models. I study multimodal representation learning, cross-modal retrieval, and hashing to improve retrieval efficiency and accuracy. For video diffusion models and large language models, I investigate sparse attention, cache reuse, reduction of redundant computation, and KV cache optimization to reduce inference latency and memory consumption.
